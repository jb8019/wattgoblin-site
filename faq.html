STRAIGHT ANSWERS.

The basics of WattGoblin, $WGBL, the creator model, the planned 10% project stake, Discord, the community mining pool and official verification.

How much did the project wallet buy at launch?

The official project wallet spent $29.58 during creation and received 8,898,168.3486 $WGBL, approximately 0.8898% of the 1 billion-token genesis supply. The transaction is disclosed as the first holding toward the planned 10% project stake / strategic reserve target.

What is WattGoblin?

WattGoblin is a creator-operated Solana community-token project built around mining, compute, hardware culture and the WattGoblin mascot. The goal is an organic community ecosystem rather than a one-day token launch.

What is the $WGBL supply?

$WGBL launched with a genesis supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens on Solana through Pump.fun. Verified on-chain burns, if performed in the future, can reduce circulating and total supply.

What is the planned 10% project stake / reserve?

The project plans to establish and maintain a project-controlled position targeting 10% of total supply — 100,000,000 $WGBL if supply is 1 billion — through creator-held tokens and planned purchases. Execution timing may vary. This is not a price floor or guaranteed market-support program.

Who owns and operates WattGoblin?

WattGoblin is creator owned and currently operated by a one-person team. The creator works a regular day job, typically 5–7 days a week, and builds WattGoblin alongside that work. The team can expand if project growth and community needs justify it.

Can creator/project revenue compensate the developer?

Yes. As disclosed on the Legal & Transparency page, creator/project revenue may compensate the developer for time, work and living expenses. Community-mining-pool rewards are specifically excluded from creator compensation.

What is the community mining pool?

It is a planned future WattGoblin mining initiative. Mining rewards attributable to the project pool are intended only for $WGBL buybacks and burns on a predetermined, publicly disclosed community schedule. They are not intended as creator income.

Do $WGBL holders receive mining rewards or yield?

No. Simply holding $WGBL does not create a right to mining-pool payouts, mining rewards, yield, dividends or revenue sharing.

Who decides when mining-pool buybacks and burns happen?

The plan is to use a published community process to predetermine the schedule and announce it in advance. Final mechanics will be published before the mining pool goes live.
