07

Community Mining Reward Policy

WattGoblin now operates a public contribution-mining initiative using the Quantus (QTC) pool on Kryptex. Mining rewards attributable to this community operation are dedicated to the published community $WGBL buyback/burn mechanism under a publicly disclosed community schedule, subject to pool/network costs and the project's published operating terms.

MINING-REWARD USE POLICY
  • community-pool mining rewards will not be used as creator salary, personal income or developer compensation;
  • creator/project compensation disclosed elsewhere on this page must come from other project revenue sources;
  • buyback/burn windows are intended to be determined through a published community process and announced in advance rather than timed around short-term market movements;
  • holding $WGBL does not create a right to mining rewards, mining-pool payouts or yield;
  • buybacks and burns do not guarantee price appreciation, liquidity or any financial return.

Current mining configuration, participation instructions and live worker telemetry are published on the Community Mining dashboard. WattGoblin intends to continue publishing reward accounting, applicable network or third-party pool fees, custody/conversion flow, completed buyback/burn execution records, and the community mechanism used to set the schedule. If operating costs are required, they should be funded or disclosed separately rather than taken as creator compensation from community mining rewards.
