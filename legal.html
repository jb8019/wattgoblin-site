Can the project operator use developer fee revenue?

Developer/creator fee revenue belongs to the project/operator unless a specific amount is separately designated for another purpose. Public statements should accurately describe any community allocation rather than implying all developer fees are restricted.

Are community mining proceeds the same as developer fees?

No. The intended structure separates future community mining activity from the official developer fee wallet. When the mining pool is operational, its wallet and fund-flow rules can be published separately.

Does a published wallet address guarantee how funds will be spent?

No. A public address provides transparency into on-chain transactions, but users should rely on clearly published policies and actual transaction history rather than assumptions.
