Nothing on WattGoblin.xyz, @WattGoblinSOL, or related community channels is investment, legal, accounting or tax advice. No statement on this website is a guarantee of price, liquidity, profit, future development or continued operation.

02

Nature of $WGBL

$WGBL is intended as a fungible Solana community token associated with the WattGoblin brand and community. It is not intended to represent stock, debt, a deposit, a fund interest, or ownership of a business.

The project does not promise that holding $WGBL will generate income, yield, dividends, interest or any other financial return. Any market price is determined by market participants and may be extremely volatile.

03

No Ownership, Profit Share or Claim on Assets

Owning $WGBL does not give a holder:
  • equity or ownership in WattGoblin or any developer-controlled business;
  • a right to creator fees, project revenue, mining revenue or other income;
  • a claim against project wallets, mining hardware, servers, GPUs, ASICs or other assets;
  • a guaranteed voting, redemption or governance right; or
  • a contractual right to future work by the developers.

05

Creator Fees & Project Revenue

The launch platform may route creator fees generated by token trading to one or more creator-designated wallets. Creator fees are variable, platform-dependent and are not guaranteed to exist, continue, or be paid successfully.

Creator-fee revenue belongs to the creator/project recipient wallet unless a different arrangement is expressly announced. Token holders do not acquire a right to that revenue merely by purchasing or holding $WGBL.

Intended Use of Creator / Project Revenue

Unless a specific allocation is later published, WattGoblin does not operate under a fixed percentage allocation. Revenue may be retained, converted, spent or reinvested at the developer's discretion for lawful purposes including the following:

03

Hardware & Compute

GPUs, ASIC miners, servers, storage, networking, power equipment and other mining or compute infrastructure.

04

Growth & Content

Artwork, media, marketing, website improvements, community tools and other brand-development expenses.

05

Community Initiatives

Lawful community events, promotions, educational experiments or other activities announced through official channels.

06

Taxes & Reserves

Taxes, professional fees, reserves, contingencies and other obligations arising from project activity.

IMPORTANT:

These categories describe possible uses of revenue, not commitments to spend a particular amount or percentage. Revenue may also remain unspent. Material allocation policies may be added later and, if adopted, will be published on official channels.
