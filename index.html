WHAT IS WATTGOBLIN?

A mascot for the part of crypto that smells like hot silicon.

WattGoblin is a Solana community token built around mining, compute, GPUs, ASICs, servers, racks, cables, power bills and the people who think all of that is fun.

It is intentionally character-driven: the goblin gives the project a recognizable identity while the community gives it a reason to exist beyond another anonymous ticker.
