PROJECT // INTENTIONS

WHY WATTGOBLIN EXISTS.

A mining-and-compute themed community token with a recognizable character, transparent official channels and room to grow into something people actually enjoy participating in.

01
THE IDEA

Build a brand first. Token second.

WattGoblin is not supposed to be an anonymous contract with a logo pasted on top. The goblin is the product identity: a character that can carry memes, mining culture, technical humor, hardware obsession and community content across the project.

The goal is to make the official project easy to recognize and difficult to impersonate: one website, one X account and one verified contract address.

01 PERSON
DEVELOPER / PROJECT OPERATOR

Creator owned. Creator operated. Built to grow organically.

WattGoblin is currently owned and operated by its creator as a one-person project. There is no large hidden development team behind the site. As the community, workload and project needs grow, the team may expand with moderators, technical contributors, developers or other support roles.

The creator also works a regular day job, typically 5–7 days per week, and is building WattGoblin alongside that employment. WattGoblin is not being presented as a get-rich-quick project. The intention is to build an organic, community-driven ecosystem, grow at a sustainable pace, and let actual participation determine what deserves to be expanded.

Creator/project revenue may compensate the developer for time, work and personal living expenses, as disclosed on the Legal & Transparency page. Mining rewards generated by the planned WattGoblin community mining pool are excluded from creator compensation.

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

What the project will not pretend to be.

01No guaranteed returns.

$WGBL is a volatile community token, not a promise of profit.

02No fake roadmap theater.

Roadmap items are goals. If priorities change, the site should say so instead of pretending otherwise.

03No unofficial contract confusion.

The contract is official only when published on WattGoblin.xyz and @WattGoblinSOL.

04Build after launch.

The project should continue producing content, experiments and community activity after the initial token launch.

COMMUNITY MINING POOL

Mining rewards belong to the community mechanism — not the creator.

The planned WattGoblin community mining pool is intended to generate mining rewards that are dedicated exclusively to scheduled $WGBL buybacks and burns under a publicly disclosed, community-determined schedule.

01COMMUNITY SCHEDULE

Buyback/burn windows will be predetermined through a published community process rather than selected around short-term price movements.

02BUYBACK + BURN ONLY

Mining rewards attributable to the project pool are intended only for $WGBL buybacks and burns, subject to published pool terms and unavoidable network/pool fees.

03NO CREATOR MINING REWARDS

The creator will not use community-pool mining rewards as salary, personal income or developer compensation.

04NO GUARANTEED PRICE EFFECT

Buybacks and burns are not a promise that $WGBL will rise in price or maintain liquidity.
