THE PROJECT

What WattGoblin is becoming.

WattGoblin is intended to grow from a Solana token and community into a broader digital-asset, mining and infrastructure experiment—without pretending those later stages already exist.

Start simple

Launch the token, publish the project identity, keep communications public, and build community participation before adding unnecessary complexity.

Build in public

Official wallets, public roadmap changes and community-facing discussions make it easier for people to independently verify what the project is actually doing.

Mining experiments

A future community mining pool may become a separate operational track. When live, it is intended to have its own community wallet and clearly defined fund flows.

Long-term interoperability

Future phases may explore liquidity, integrations and cross-chain accessibility where technically and economically justified.
