JOIN COMMUNITY MINING

Point your miner at the Goblin Grid.

No WattGoblin email/password is required. Contributors connect directly to Kryptex Pool using the public WattGoblin mining identifier plus a unique worker name. Mining this way contributes rewards to the WattGoblin community mining account; it does not create an individual payout claim.

IMPORTANT

Use a unique worker name so you can identify your rig on the public leaderboard. Never share your wallet seed phrase, private keys, Kryptex login, or any personal account password.

HOW THIS IS STRUCTURED

Public contribution mining, not a holder yield program.

01Kryptex provides the pool infrastructure.

WattGoblin is currently organizing a community mining operation on Kryptex; it is not claiming to operate independent stratum infrastructure.

02Workers contribute to one community mining account.

Unique worker names make contributions visible, but they do not create an individual claim to pool balances or treasury funds.

03Community mining rewards stay separate from creator compensation.

The published policy reserves community-mining rewards for community-scheduled $WGBL buyback/burn activity, subject to disclosed network/pool costs.

04No guaranteed price effect or return.

Mining, buybacks and burns do not guarantee $WGBL price appreciation, liquidity, or any return to token holders or miners.
