THE WATTGOBLIN ROADMAP

From token launch to a broader ecosystem.

This roadmap describes intended direction, not guaranteed milestones or exchange listings. Timing and scope can change based on technical feasibility, resources, market conditions and community participation.

PHASE I

Foundation & pre-launch

Website, project identity, legal/transparency material, official X presence, GitHub Discussions, official developer fee wallet and launch readiness.

PHASE II

Solana launch & early community

Launch $WGBL through the selected Solana launch path, establish on-chain references, and focus on organic community growth and public communication.

PHASE III

Liquidity & visibility

Support healthy market infrastructure where available, improve project discoverability, and pursue third-party data/indexing integrations when requirements are met.

PHASE IV

Community programs

Introduce small community activities such as token raffles, hashrate experiments or other participation events when they can be operated responsibly and transparently.

PHASE V

Community mining pool

Develop and test the community mining-pool concept. At launch of that system, create a separate community wallet and publicly define how mining proceeds are handled.

PHASE VI

Broader integrations

Evaluate wallet, DEX, analytics and ecosystem integrations. Explore accessibility beyond the initial Solana venue only when technically sensible.

PHASE VII

Cross-chain experimentation

Explore Base or other cross-chain mechanisms if there is a real user or liquidity reason to do so, with clear documentation of risks and mechanics.

PHASE VIII

Mining, compute & infrastructure

Longer-term experiments may extend into mining hardware, compute, infrastructure and related digital-asset systems as resources and community participation grow.
